HOWBETTER · PRIVACY

Your privacy choices.

Updated November 6, 2026. Howbetter provides an independent game page and notes. OnlineGames.io supplies the embedded Monster Survivors game, developed by FreezeNova.

Before you load the external game

The game frame is not requested until you choose “Load external game”. Your browser then contacts OnlineGames.io and services used by that provider. These can include advertising, cookies, local storage, and game progress data. They operate separately from our optional Google Analytics.

Read OnlineGames.io’s privacy policy before loading. The provider’s own privacy controls apply within its frame. Howbetter cannot inspect, export, or delete the game’s saved progress or provider cookies.

Reload or close this page to stop it from loading the game again. A new page load returns to the unloaded placeholder. This does not delete data already held by the provider.

Optional Google Analytics

Our Google Analytics tag stays off unless you accept it. Reading the notes and loading the external game still work if you decline. You can change your choice through “Analytics preferences” in the footer. This choice controls Google Analytics only; it does not turn off Cloudflare’s separate measurement or the external game provider’s services.

  • With permission, we send page views containing the page title and path, without URL query strings.
  • We record when the game frame is requested or reloaded. These are intentions, not proof that the game started, a person played, or a run finished.
  • Optional guide feedback sends only “yes” or “no” and the guide identifier. We do not send free-text comments, email addresses or game saves in these events.
  • Google also processes browser and device information. Our analytics cookies are configured to expire after 90 days without extending expiry on each visit. Google signals and advertising-personalization settings are disabled in our tag.

Your Google Analytics choice is stored in this browser as guide-analytics-choice-v1. If storage is unavailable, our Google Analytics stays off. Withdrawing permission disables that tag, removes the Google Analytics cookies this site sets, and reloads the page. That can interrupt an external game in progress. It does not automatically remove previously processed data or disable Cloudflare measurement.

See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Cloudflare hosting and performance measurement

Cloudflare serves this website and can process network and security information needed to deliver and protect it. The production website also uses Cloudflare’s Web Analytics/RUM beacon for page and browser-performance measurements, including load timing and Core Web Vitals.

Cloudflare can add this beacon to the delivered page independently of our Google Analytics tag. It can run and send measurements before you make a Google Analytics choice, including when you decline Google Analytics. The Google Analytics controls above do not turn it off.

Cloudflare describes its RUM measurement as using the current page’s browser-performance data rather than browser storage such as cookies or localStorage. This does not mean that all requests to this website wait for consent, or that measurement has no processing at all. Hosting requests and RUM measurements also do not establish the number of real players.

Read Cloudflare’s data collection documentation, RUM privacy information, and privacy policy for details.

Advertising

Howbetter currently does not load its own advertising tag. The external provider can display ads after you load its game. Our analytics permission does not authorize or control those ads, and they are not reported here as Howbetter advertising revenue.
