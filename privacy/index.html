HOWBETTER · PRIVACY

Your choices.
Two separate services.

Updated November 6, 2026. Howbetter provides an independent game page and notes. OnlineGames.io supplies the embedded Monster Survivors game, developed by FreezeNova.

Before you load the external game

The game frame is not requested until you choose “Load external game”. Your browser then contacts OnlineGames.io and services used by that provider. These can include advertising, cookies, local storage, and game progress data. They operate separately from our optional Google Analytics.

Read OnlineGames.io’s privacy policy before loading. The provider’s own privacy controls apply within its frame. Howbetter cannot inspect, export, or delete the game’s saved progress or provider cookies.

Reload or close this page to stop it from loading the game again. A new page load returns to the unloaded placeholder. This does not delete data already held by the provider.

Our optional analytics

Our Google Analytics tag stays off unless you accept it. Reading the notes and loading the external game still work if you decline. You can change your choice through “Analytics preferences” in the footer.

  • With permission, we send page views containing the page title and path, without URL query strings.
  • We record when the game frame is requested or reloaded. These are intentions, not proof that the game started, a person played, or a run finished.
  • Optional guide feedback sends only “yes” or “no” and the guide identifier. We do not send free-text comments, email addresses or game saves in these events.
  • Google also processes browser and device information. Our analytics cookies are configured to expire after 90 days without extending expiry on each visit. Google signals and advertising-personalization settings are disabled in our tag.

Your choice is stored in this browser as guide-analytics-choice-v1. If storage is unavailable, our analytics stays off. Withdrawing permission disables our tag, removes the analytics cookies this site sets, and reloads the page. That can interrupt an external game in progress. It does not automatically remove previously processed data.

See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Hosting and advertising

Cloudflare serves this website and can process network and security information needed to deliver and protect it. Hosting statistics can include automated requests and do not establish the number of real players. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

Howbetter currently does not load its own advertising tag. The external provider can display ads after you load its game. Our analytics permission does not authorize or control those ads, and they are not reported here as Howbetter advertising revenue.
